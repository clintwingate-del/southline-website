What Makes a Site Viable for Data Center Development?

Critical Requirements

  • Adequate power capacity (5-50+ MW depending on scale)
  • Proximity to robust fiber infrastructure
  • Appropriate zoning (industrial/special use)
  • Structural capacity (600+ lbs/SF floor loading)
  • Adequate cooling capacity or space for CRAC units
  • Security and controlled access capability
  • Clear height (12-18+ feet for raised floor)

Chicago Advantages

  • Major midwest data hub location
  • Competitive power costs vs. coasts
  • Natural cooling climate (lower operating costs)
  • Strong fiber carrier presence
  • Available industrial properties
  • Illinois data center tax incentives
  • Low natural disaster risk

Target Chicago Suburbs for Data Center Development

Based on power infrastructure, fiber availability, and zoning, the following suburbs offer strong data center site potential:

  • Elk Grove Village: Established industrial hub, excellent fiber, strong power infrastructure, data center-friendly zoning.
  • Aurora: Growing tech presence, available industrial properties, competitive power rates, fiber expansion.
  • Joliet: Lower land costs, strong industrial base, available power capacity, interstate highway access.
  • Romeoville: Strategic logistics location, fiber availability, industrial zoning, competitive utilities.
  • Schaumburg: Corporate corridor location, premium fiber infrastructure, established tech ecosystem.
  • Naperville: High-quality location, strong infrastructure, corporate tenant proximity, premium positioning.
