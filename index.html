Midwest Utility-Scale Battery Storage Project

50 MW Battery Storage

Challenge: Solar developer needed to add 50 MW battery storage to existing solar project to qualify for Illinois CRGA Coal-to-Solar Grant Program ($5.5M potential grant). Project faced complex ComEd interconnection requirements and municipal permitting in jurisdiction with no prior energy storage approvals.

Result: Secured special use permit in 7 months • Navigated ComEd interconnection queue • Positioned for $5.5M CRGA grant award

Solution: Developed comprehensive municipal education strategy on battery storage safety, coordinated early ComEd system impact study, prepared NFPA 855-compliant fire safety plans, and structured grant application timeline to align with municipal approval process.

Community Solar + Storage Hybrid Project

Illinois Shines + CRGA

Challenge: Community solar developer wanted to add battery storage to 2 MW solar project to increase project value and qualify for additional CRGA incentives. Needed to navigate both Illinois Shines program requirements and local zoning for storage component while maintaining community solar approval.

Result: Approved as accessory use (no additional permits) • Qualified for DG Rebate + VPP enrollment • 4 months total timeline

Solution: Positioned battery storage as "accessory use" to approved Illinois Shines solar farm, eliminating need for separate special use permit. Coordinated Illinois Power Agency approval for combined solar+storage project and facilitated VPP program enrollment for ongoing revenue stream.

Commercial Warehouse Peak Shaving Installation

C&I Battery Storage

Challenge: 500,000 sq ft industrial warehouse in Aurora faced $15,000+/month demand charges from ComEd. Owner wanted to install 500 kW/2 MWh battery system but needed to navigate complex fire code requirements, building permits, and utility coordination.

Result: Building permit approved in 6 weeks • Fire marshal sign-off achieved • Projected $120K annual savings on ComEd demand charges

Solution: Prepared NFPA 855 compliant fire safety documentation, coordinated fire department pre-installation walkthrough, managed ComEd interconnection notification, and structured system to qualify for future VPP program participation for additional revenue.
