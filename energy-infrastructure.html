Why Illinois Energy Storage Projects Need Specialized Support

Battery storage projects face unique regulatory challenges that require deep local expertise:

  • Municipal Approvals: Special use permits required in most jurisdictions, often involving public hearings and community concerns about noise, safety, and visual impact.
  • Utility Interconnection: Complex ComEd and Ameren processes requiring technical documentation, impact studies, and upgrade negotiations.
  • Fire and Safety: Coordination with fire marshals on emergency response plans, setback requirements, and safety systems (NFPA 855 compliance).
  • Environmental Review: IEPA permits, stormwater management, and wetland/floodplain considerations.
  • Community Engagement: Proactive outreach to address concerns and build local support before formal approval processes.
